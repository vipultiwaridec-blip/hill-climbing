## 🎮 About The Game

**Hill Climb Racing** is a browser-based 2D driving game where your goal is simple:

> 🏁 Drive as far as you can without crashing!

Control your vehicle across challenging hills, maintain your balance, collect points, and try to achieve your highest score.

The game focuses on simple controls, physics-based movement, and an enjoyable arcade-style racing experience.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🚗 Physics-based vehicle movement
- ⛰️ Dynamic hill terrain
- 🎮 Smooth keyboard controls
- 💰 Collectible coins
- 🏆 Score system
- ⛽ Fuel/energy system
- 💥 Vehicle crash mechanics
- 📈 Increasing difficulty
- 🔄 Restart and replay functionality
- 📱 Responsive game interface
- 🌐 Runs directly in a web browser

---

## 🕹️ Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| `⬆️ Arrow Up` | Accelerate |
| `⬇️ Arrow Down` | Brake / Reverse |
| `⬅️ Arrow Left` | Rotate vehicle backward |
| `➡️ Arrow Right` | Rotate vehicle forward |
| `R` | Restart Game |

> 💡 Keep the vehicle balanced while climbing steep hills!

---

## 🎯 Objective

Your mission is to:

1. 🚗 Drive your vehicle across the terrain.
2. ⛰️ Climb as many hills as possible.
3. 💰 Collect coins and bonuses.
4. ⛽ Manage your fuel.
5. 💥 Avoid crashing.
6. 🏆 Beat your previous high score.

The farther you travel, the higher your score!

---

## 🧠 Game Mechanics

### 🚗 Vehicle Physics

The vehicle reacts to the terrain and uses physics-based movement to create a more realistic hill-climbing experience.

### ⛰️ Terrain

The track contains hills, slopes, and uneven terrain that require careful control.

### 💰 Coins

Collect coins while driving to increase your score.

### ⛽ Fuel

Keep an eye on your fuel level. Running out of fuel can end your journey.

### 💥 Crash System

Landing badly or flipping the vehicle can result in a crash.
